Cooper Flagg

Height: 6'9" | Weight: 205 lbs | College: Duke | Drafted: 1st Overall, 2025

Background

Cooper Flagg was born on December 21, 2006, and grew up in Newport, Maine. Flagg first gained fame while playing at Nokomis Regional High School before transferring to Montverde Academy in Florida.

Flagg rose to become one of the top high school basketball players in the nation. Still, at Montverde, he evolved into a versatile player who could impact a game in multiple ways. Often seen as a once in a generation talent.

After High School, Flagg went to Duke University for the 2024–25 season. In his one season at Duke, Flagg starred as a freshman and had one of the best campaigns by a first-year player in recent memory. This was where he cemented himself as a generational talent while competing against a stacked draft class.

Flagg averaged 19.2 points, 7.5 rebounds, 4.2 assists, 1.4 steals, and 1.4 blocks per game while helping Duke reach the Final Four. Flagg also earned National Player of the Year awards.

Playing Style

Two-Way Point Forward

I would characterize Cooper Flagg as a two-way point forward.

The designation speaks for itself, meaning that the player can impact the game on both sides of the ball and operate in a position as a point forward. Two-way players are those who provide value on both ends of the floor. Although some characteristics of his playstyle are yet to be fully developed.

The name "point forward" typically refers to a larger forward with the ball-handling and playmaking abilities of a traditional point guard. They're able to push the offense, create playmaking opportunities and shoot off the dribble.

Flagg can be characterized as both a two-way player and a point forward. Offensively, Flagg has a comfortability creating his scoring opportunities from the perimeter but can also attack the basket to finish on the break or create a shot for a teammate due to this ability to draw defenders in.

Flagg's unique skill set is also complemented by his passing ability, given his size and wingspan, making it challenging for some opponents to contest or even see the passes in transition.

Defensively, Flagg's size, agility, length, and basketball IQ enable him to protect the rim, contest or block shots while being nimble enough to switch onto smaller perimeter players. He also has the grit and effort needed on the defensive side, something that many players can't be taught.

Offensive Skills

Finishing

One of Flagg's offensive skills that I find most intriguing is his ability to finish around the rim. During Flagg's rookie campaign, he shot approximately 74% on layups and shots within three feet of the basket.

Flagg's height and athleticism combine to finish over defenders, and he has displayed good body control to score with his back to the basket, even when he cannot finish with a traditional layup or dunk.

Additionally, Flagg has demonstrated that he can be a threat in transition and can score without a dribble, so he can finish on the break with the ball or create an opportunity for a teammate.

Shot Creation

Flagg has the ability to create shots for himself and finish around the rim. Moreover, Flagg has strong ball-handling skills, making him a threat to score when attacking the basket one-on-one.

Flagg can utilize his size to create space against smaller defenders. He can also get to the middle of the court and finish or kick-out to an open teammate.

Most of Flagg's shot attempts have been located inside the three-point line, which speaks to his game being more built around finishing at the rim than shooting from long-range.

Passing

Flagg is a playmaker who can impact the game on both ends of the floor. He averaged 4.5 assists per game as a rookie, which was extraordinary for a 6-foot-9 player.

Flagg can make plays for his teammates with the ball in his hands, utilizing his strength and playmaking vision. His ability to score and create for others makes him a challenging match for defenders who do not know if he will drive to the basket or kick the ball out to an open teammate.

Shooting

Shooting is an area of Flagg's offensive game that needs improvement, particularly from long distance.

Flagg was never known as a three-point shooter at Duke, and his rookie season saw his three-point shooting percentage drop to 29.5%, far below his three-point shooting percentage of 38.5% at Duke. Which can be explained by simply struggling to adjust to a faster, more physical and more demanding style of play within the NBA.

His percentage of made shots from farther away show the areas he still has to work on in order to fully complete his skill set within the NBA.

If Flagg can develop into a consistent three-point maker, it will force the defense to respect his shot from long range and create better opportunities when driving, making it even harder to help side defense to collapse.

Road to the NBA

The Dallas Mavericks picked up Cooper Flagg with the first overall pick of the 2025 NBA Draft.

Flagg came out of the NBA draft at just 18 years old but was immediately thrust into a significant role. Flagg's size and versatility enabled Dallas to utilize him as more than just a traditional forward, and Flagg could handle the ball, create scoring opportunities, attack, rebound, and defend multiple positions.

Defensive Skills

Defensively, Flagg has the ability to impact the game in several ways. Flagg averaged 1.2 steals and 0.9 blocks per game during his rookie season, and his defensive production was on display for many fans during his Rookie of the Year campaign.

His height enables him to guard and protect the paint, but his agility is also a defensive strength, as he can switch onto perimeter players to keep them from easily scoring.

Flagg's defensive versatility helps him guard multiple positions, and he can affect shots on the interior and contest perimeter jumpers.

Moreover, Flagg can rebound, which helps him prolong possessions and provide his team with immediate offense.

Strengths

Versatility

One of Flagg's most significant strengths is his overall game versatility. Flagg can score, playmake, defend, pass, and rebound.

Interior Scoring

Flagg is a gifted inside scorer who utilizes his size and athletic ability to score around the basket.

Playmaking

While Flagg may be known more for his scoring ability, he can also create opportunities for his teammates with his playmaking. Flagg averaged 4.5 assists per game as a rookie.

Defense

Flagg's defensive versatility has been highlighted by his ability to steal the ball, block shots, rebound, and guard multiple positions.

Size

Flagg's height and skill set present matchup problems for many defenders. While smaller players will be unable to guard him, larger players may struggle with his ball-handling and perimeter skills.

Weaknesses

Three-Point Shooting

One of Flagg's most evident weaknesses from his rookie season was his inconsistency from long distance. Flagg shot just 29.5% from three-point range.

Therefore, the Mavericks should not expect to utilize Flagg's three-point shooting much until he proves to be a consistent marksman.

Long-Range Shot Selection

Flagg was much more efficient from inside the arc than beyond it, and he will need to improve his shot selection to become a reliable scorer for the Mavericks.

Turnovers

Flagg averaged 2.3 turnovers per game, and many of them were unforced. While the Mavericks may ask Flagg to handle the ball to create offense, he will have to be much more mindful of his turnovers.

Experience

Flagg had minimal experience before joining the Mavericks. Therefore, it is expected that some of his weaknesses will be ironed out through his experience in the NBA.

He played for Duke as previously said where he also had an all-time college career.

Player Archetype

Two-Way Point Forward

Cooper Flagg's optimal player archetype is a two-way point forward.

Flagg has the size of a forward, but he can also create scores for himself and others like a point guard. Furthermore, Flagg has the defensive versatility to impact the game on both ends of the floor.

He does not necessarily have one dominant skill, but he impacts the game in many ways.
