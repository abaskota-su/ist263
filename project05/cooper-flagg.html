Background

Cooper Flagg was born on December 21, 2006, and grew up in Newport, Maine.

He played at Nokomis Regional High School before transferring to Montverde Academy in Florida.

Flagg later attended Duke University. In his one season at Duke, he averaged 19.2 points, 7.5 rebounds, 4.2 assists, 1.4 steals, and 1.4 blocks per game.

He also helped Duke reach the Final Four and won National Player of the Year.

Road to the NBA

The Dallas Mavericks selected Cooper Flagg with the first overall pick in the 2025 NBA Draft.

Flagg entered the NBA at only 18 years old but was immediately given a big role.

His size and versatility allowed Dallas to use him as more than a normal forward because he could handle the ball, pass, rebound, score, and defend.

Playing Style

Two-Way Point Forward

I would characterize Cooper Flagg as a two-way point forward.

He has the size of a forward but can handle the ball and create offense similar to a guard. He can also make an impact on defense.

What stands out to me the most is how many different things he can do on the court.

Offensive Skills

Finishing

Flagg is very good around the basket. His size and athleticism make him difficult to stop once he gets into the paint.

Shot Creation

Flagg can create his own shot off the dribble and use his size against smaller defenders.

Passing

Flagg averaged 4.5 assists per game as a rookie. When defenders help on his drives, he is good at finding an open teammate.

Shooting

Shooting is probably his biggest area for improvement. Flagg shot 29.5% from three-point range during his rookie season.

If he becomes a better three-point shooter, his driving game could become even more dangerous.

Defensive Skills

Flagg can defend both inside and on the perimeter. He averaged 1.2 steals and 0.9 blocks per game.

His size and quickness allow him to defend multiple positions.

The biggest thing I would want to see improve is his three-point shot.
